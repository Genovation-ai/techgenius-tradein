## A note on the CC BY-SA files

Share-alike asks that adaptations of the image be released under the same
licence. Showing a photograph on a product page is not an adaptation of it, so
the practical obligation is the same as the CC BY files above: name the author
and the licence. Do not crop, recolour or composite these particular files into
new artwork without checking that condition — the CC BY ones carry no such
restriction, and your own photographs carry none at all.

Source: Wikimedia Commons — article infobox images, and per-device categories
(`Category:Samsung Galaxy S20 FE` and similar), which name one model and are
curated, so they avoid the family lineups and wrong variants that plain search
returns.
